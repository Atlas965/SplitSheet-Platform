Phase 2: Scaling Features for Incubator Readiness
Objective: Develop advanced features that position SplitSheet as a comprehensive music rights management platform, demonstrating scalability and innovation for incubator programs.

2.1. Industry-Standard Compliance & Enhanced Audit Trail
Instructions:

	1.	PRO Integration: Develop functionality to allow users to specify their PRO affiliation and CAE/IPI numbers within their profile and for each collaborator in an agreement. Explore options for direct export formats compatible with major PROs (e.g., SOCAN, ASCAP, BMI).
	2.	Standard Identifiers: Make ISRC and ISWC fields mandatory for relevant agreement types (e.g., split sheets) to ensure comprehensive metadata collection.
	3.	Legal Audit Trail Enhancement: Augment the existing “Activity Log” to capture more granular details for every signature, including:
	◦	IP addresses.
	◦	Browser fingerprints (user agent strings).
	◦	Timestamp with timezone information.
	◦	This data should be securely stored and easily retrievable for legal verification.

2.2. The
“Rights Ledger” Expansion

Instructions:

	1.	Real-time Rights Management Dashboard: Enhance the `ownership.tsx` page to evolve into a comprehensive “Rights Ledger” dashboard. This should visually represent:
	◦	Automated Payout Calculations: Display real-time calculations of what each collaborator is owed based on `revenueEvents` and `payoutRecords`.
	◦	Historical Ownership: Visualize changes in ownership over time, leveraging the append-only nature of `ownershipRecords`.
	◦	Revenue Streams: Clearly show revenue breakdown by source (streaming, sync, performance, mechanical).
	2.	Dispute Resolution Workflow: Implement a structured workflow for collaborators to dispute or negotiate splits before an agreement is finalized. This could involve:
	◦	A dedicated negotiation interface.
	◦	Version control for proposed splits.
	◦	Notification system for pending disputes.

2.3. Traction & Growth Tools
Instructions:

	1.	Collaborator Viral Loop: Optimize the “Signer” experience for non-users to encourage conversion into “Creators.”
	◦	Seamless Onboarding: When a non-user signs an agreement, provide a clear, low-friction path to create their own SplitSheet account and start their first agreement.
	◦	Value Proposition: Clearly articulate the benefits of becoming a “Creator” (e.g., managing all their agreements, tracking royalties).
	2.	Analytics for Labels/Managers: Develop a “Label View” or “Manager Dashboard” feature.
	◦	This view should allow users with appropriate roles (e.g., “admin” or “manager” in `users` table) to oversee all agreements and rights across their entire roster of artists/producers from a single, consolidated dashboard.
	◦	Include aggregated analytics on total agreements, pending signatures, and revenue distribution across their managed artists.

Objective: Implement overarching improvements to enhance user experience, application robustness, and brand consistency.

Instructions:

	1.	Consistent Branding: Ensure that “SplitSheet” and its logo are consistently displayed and styled across all pages. Update the browser tab title to reflect the application name accurately.

For scenarios like unauthorized access, provide clear explanations and actionable options (e.g., “You need to be logged in to view this page. Please log in or create an account.”).
