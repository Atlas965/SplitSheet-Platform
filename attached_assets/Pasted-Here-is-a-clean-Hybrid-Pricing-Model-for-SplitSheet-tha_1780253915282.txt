SplitSheet Hybrid Pricing Model

Overview

SplitSheet uses a hybrid pricing structure combining:

* Session-based payments (primary creator-facing model)
* Subscription tiers (recurring SaaS revenue layer)
* Enterprise licensing (scale + institutional adoption)

This structure aligns with real music industry behavior where collaborations happen per project, but professional users require ongoing workflows and infrastructure.

⸻

🎵 1. Free Tier — Starter Access

$0

Designed for onboarding and viral adoption.

Includes:

* 1 collaboration project
* Up to 2 contributors
* Basic split creation tool
* Contributor confirmation links
* Timestamped agreement summary
* PDF export

Purpose:

* Reduce friction
* Enable first experience
* Drive network effects

Revenue Logic Summary

SplitSheet revenue is generated through three layers:

1. Transaction Layer (Session Fees)
    * Core usage monetization
    * Aligns with project-based creation behavior
2. Subscription Layer (Recurring SaaS)
    * Converts heavy users into predictable monthly revenue
3. Enterprise Layer (Infrastructure Licensing)
    * High-value contracts with industry institutions

⸻

Strategic Rationale

* Music collaboration is project-based → supports session pricing
* Professional usage is recurring → supports subscriptions
* Industry adoption is institutional → supports enterprise licensing

This hybrid structure ensures:

* Low friction onboarding
* High conversion potential
* Long-term scalable SaaS revenue
